# AI Assessment Module

**Status**: Future Phase

## Purpose
Leverages AI (e.g., computer vision) to assess athlete performance, technique, and form from uploaded video content. Provides automated feedback and scoring.

## Expected Scope
- Video upload and processing pipeline.
- AI result visualization and feedback.
- Progress tracking over time.

## Architecture Notes
- Will require robust backend integration for video processing.
- Frontend needs complex media player controls with annotation capabilities.
