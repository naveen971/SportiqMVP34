# Government Module

**Status**: Future Phase

## Purpose
The Government module provides high-level oversight, reporting, and analytics for government sports officials to track regional sports participation, talent development, and event outcomes.

## Expected Scope
- High-level overview dashboard.
- Analytics and reporting tools.
- Talent identification pipelines.
- Policy and announcement distribution.

## Architecture Notes
- Heavily dependent on the future Analytics module.
- Requires strict data privacy and aggregated data views.
