# SportIQ

## Vision
SportIQ aims to digitize and elevate the sports ecosystem by creating a unified platform that connects athletes, coaches, organizers, and government officials. It simplifies talent discovery, event management, and athletic development.

## Problem
Currently, sports management and athletic progression are fragmented. Athletes struggle with visibility, coaches lack streamlined evaluation tools, organizers face logistical nightmares, and government officials lack data-driven insights for grassroots development.

## Solution
A comprehensive, unified application that centralizes player profiles, coaching metrics, event management, and administrative oversight, providing real-time data and tools tailored to every stakeholder in the sports ecosystem.

## Users

### Athlete
Focuses on skill progression, showcasing talent, finding opportunities, and tracking performance over time.

### Coach
Needs tools to evaluate athletes, manage team strategies, track progress, and communicate effectively with players.

### Organiser
Requires streamlined event creation, scheduling, ticketing, participant management, and real-time tournament operations.

### Government Official
Needs macro-level data and reports to analyze grassroots development, allocate funding, and track the overall health of regional sports programs.

## MVP
The Minimum Viable Product focuses on core onboarding, basic role-based dashboards, simple event listing, and fundamental athlete profile creation to validate the concept and onboard early adopters.

## Future Scope
Integration with wearables for real-time biometric tracking, AI-driven performance analytics, automated video highlight generation, and advanced monetization pathways for athletes and organizers.

## Product Philosophy
- User-Centric: Every feature must solve a real problem for the specific user role.
- Simplicity first: Complex data should be presented simply and intuitively.
- Scalability: The architecture must support future growth without significant rewrites.
- Performance: Fast load times and offline capabilities for users in varying network conditions.
